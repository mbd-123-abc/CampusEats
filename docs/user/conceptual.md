## What is Campus Eats 

### Motivation

College students are busy. Between back-to-back lectures and everything else, eating well is often the first thing to slip. This has more drastic impacts than freshmen realize. For me, it was a dramatic nosebleed, a blood test, and the diagnosis of anemia. Anemia isn’t just a medical term; It’s the random headaches, breathlessness climbing Odegaard’s stairs, and the sudden realisation I haven’t eaten since morning. It’s watching your GPA thrive while your health suffers because eating feels like one task too many. `CampusEats` is more than an app; it’s the safety net I wish I’d had.

### How it Works

Born at the University of Washington, `Campus Eats` is a mobile app designed to eliminate the decision fatigue of college dining. It is the first intelligent dining companion built to sync with the chaotic rhythm of student life, weaving together student schedules, dietary nutrition goals, and campus geography. Campus Eats connects your Google Calendar, finds the gaps in your day, and schedules each meal. It recommends the closest campus dining venue, suggesting the specific meal that best match your preferances and nutrient deficits.

## Why use Campus Eats

### Features

- **Dashboard** - hero card showing your next meal, specific recommendation, one-tap directions, and one-tap menu filtered by time of day (incase you don't like what it suggested)
- **Timeline** - horizontal scroll of your meal times (in red) and other activities (in grey) for the day
- **Nutrient Pulse** - animated rings tracking your daily nutrients (select from iron, protein, vitamin D, calcium, B12, and fiber)
- **Meal Logging** - USDA-powered food logging for mood and nutrition tracking 
- **Profile** - set preferences (dietary restrictions, likes/dislikes, nutrients to focus on, dining plan, walking speed, academic intensity)

## How Things are Chosen 

### Meals 

- **Safety First** - Allergens and hard restrictions (vegan, halal, kosher, etc.) will never be present in a suggested meal
- **Absorption** - When it comes to macro and micro nutrients, some are best absorbed when paired with others (eg. iron w/vitamin c). So if you are low on a nutrient towards the end of the day, the app will prioritize combination meals
- **Reality Checks** - The app will only suggest meals you can reach, get your food from, and eat within the given time-frame. Our app doesn't yet account for online ordering (future feature)
- **Preference** - Preferences will always be taken into account. There is no way to turn it off, but you can always change it 
 
### Times 
- **One Time cCnnect** - You only have to connect your Google Calendar once. Keep in mind this means there are no re-connect options if you connect the wrong account. 
- **Calendar Magic** - The app will automatically find gaps in your day daily (also re-calibrating if your calendar changes between the day)
- **Buffers** - A 20 minute gap does not mean 20 minutes of walking, neither does it mean 20 minutes of walking. The app will optimize for locations on the way (on the path), and if you need to come back then locations closest to you (eg. a place 5 minutes away)
